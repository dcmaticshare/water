How HeatCover works.

01

Dose automatically

The dosing system introduces HeatCover into the pool circulation.

02

Distribute through the water

The circulation system spreads the liquid throughout the pool.

03

Form the surface barrier

The liquid naturally rises to form an extremely thin, invisible surface layer.

04

Repeat each day

Programmed dosing maintains the intended treatment routine.
