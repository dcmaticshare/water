Cleaning the filter bed
Water backwashing reverses flow through the bed and carries released dirt to waste. In a compatible air-assisted installation, an air-scour stage first loosens deposits, followed by water backwashing and a final rinse before filtration restarts.

Optional air-assisted operation
The diagram is a proposed sequence for a suitably designed system. Air supply, pressure limits and the air-clearance path need to match the selected filter; trapped air must be cleared before restarting filtration. Air-assisted equipment is not implied to be included with every filter.

Pressure-based monitoring
Optional pressure measurements before and after the filter reveal pressure loss. A rising differential pressure at the same flow rate can help guide backwash decisions. The readings in the diagram are illustrative, not cleaning thresholds.
